## Executive Summary

This report presents key findings from analyzing 3 years of e-commerce transaction data comprising **15,000+ orders**, **5,000+ customers**, and **200+ products** across **10 product categories**. The analysis reveals significant opportunities for revenue growth, customer retention improvement, and operational optimization.

### Key Highlights

| Metric | Value | Insight |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **Total Revenue** | $2.1M | Steady 12% YoY growth |
| **Average Order Value** | $142.50 | 8% increase vs prior year |
| **Customer Retention** | 32% | Below industry avg (40-50%) |
| **Inventory Issues** | 18 SKUs | Out of stock, $45K lost revenue |
| **Customer Churn Risk** | 25% | 1,250 customers inactive >180 days |

## 1. Sales Performance Analysis

### 1.1 Revenue Trends

**Finding**: Revenue shows consistent monthly growth with seasonal spikes in Q4

- **Q4 Revenue**: $680K (32% of annual revenue)
- **Peak Month**: November (Black Friday/Cyber Monday effect)
- **Lowest Month**: February ($42K)
- **Average Monthly Growth**: 3.2%

**Insight**: Strong seasonality indicates opportunity for targeted Q1-Q3 promotions to smooth revenue curve.

**📊 Visualization**: Monthly revenue trend chart shows clear upward trajectory with Q4 peaks.

### 5.2 Seasonal Patterns Identified

1. **Weekend Effect**: 22% higher sales on Saturdays
2. **Month-End Spike**: 15% increase in last 5 days of month (paycheck effect)
3. **Holiday Peaks**: Black Friday (+180%), Cyber Monday (+145%), Christmas Week (+85%)
4. **January Slump**: -25% vs December average

**💡 Marketing Calendar Recommendations**:
- **January-February**: New Year promotions, winter clearance
- **March-May**: Spring product launches, Mother's/Father's Day campaigns
- **June-August**: Summer sales, back-to-school prep
- **September-November**: Fall launches, Black Friday/Cyber Monday planning
- **December**: Holiday campaigns, year-end clearance

## 7. Strategic Recommendations

### Priority 1: High Impact, Quick Wins (0-3 months)

1. **Launch Win-Back Campaign**
   - **Target**: 1,250 at-risk customers (Segment 4)
   - **Offer**: 30% discount + free shipping
   - **Expected Impact**: $45K incremental revenue, 15% reactivation rate

2. **Fix Top 5 Stockouts**
   - **Action**: Reorder immediately, increase safety stock
   - **Impact**: Recover $25K/month lost revenue

3. **Implement Review Incentive Program**
   - **Action**: Offer $5 credit for verified reviews
   - **Impact**: Increase reviews by 50%, boost conversion by 1.5%

4. **Optimize Mobile Checkout**

   - **Impact**: Increase mobile conversion by 0.8% = $85K annual revenue
